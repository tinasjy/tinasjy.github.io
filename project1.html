Rainwater Collection System

Overview

The goal of this project was to design and model a functional Rainwater Collection System for individual households in Van Anda where people live off the grid a large portion of the year.

What I Did

  • Built a simulation in Microsoft Excel to perform simulations for a rooftop rainwater collection system including collection area, historical rainfall data, energy systems, pumps, environmental impact, maintenance, and total cost.
  • Peformed Stakeholder Consultation to generate target design requirements so that the final design would fit with the unique needs of Van Anda's residents.

    • We played around with multiple combinations of paramaters in our Microsoft Excel simulation to figure out which set would bring the highest stakeholder satisfaction. These paramaters include the power system, type of pump, number and size of filters, disinfection system, catchment tank and storage container sizes.

    I worked on the health and safety risk exposure section, using a formula to evaluate the pros and cons of different aspects of the model. For example, how using diesel rather than solar panels for energy would bring about a higher safety risk as more maintenance for refills/cleaning must happen.

    Additionally, I focused on the maintenance criteria for our satisfaction curves. I used daily consumption to calculate the number of filter changes required, and the days they occur on. I then added maintenance operations due to the pump, solar/diesel energy refills and seasonal cleaning.

    What I Learned

  • I learned how to use Microsoft Excel to build a simulation involving multiple interconnected parts and gained a better understanding of how to break complex tasks into more manageable parts.
  • I also learned that the "ideal design" is never the most general solution, instead it must be very specific as to meet the unique circumstances of the stakeholders.
