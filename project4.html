Cardboard Chair

Overview

The goal of this project was to design and develop a functional cardboard chair for natural disaster relief. When natural disasters happen without warning, people are required to evacuate in large groups. Our cardboard chair "Critter Comfort" eases this transition for young kids by providing them with a Cardboard chair, that acts both as furniture and as entertainment, with its built in ring toss and tic-tac-toe. It is also easy on parents and volunteers as it is easy to set up and provides storage underneath the seat.
