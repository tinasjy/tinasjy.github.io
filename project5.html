Protected Bike Lanes

Overview

The goal of this project was to produce a reccomendation for building a new protected bike lane in Vancouver. We reviewed and analyzed five different locations: The Greenway Project, West Kent, West 14th Avenue, West Kingsway and East Kinsway

We started by identifying the main stakeholders using a saliency model, and then analyzed the causes and effects of bike lanes on multiple different scales using a Causal Loop Diagram. After ensuring the five locations would meet design requirememts, we went on to create target design specifications.

Skills & Technologies
