Copper Mining

Overview

The goal of this project was to analyze and reccomend the next copper mining site for Coppernicus Mining Ltd. in Vancouver.

We first consulted essential stakeholders and proposed a solution for continuous stakeholder engagement. This included the Mining Company, BC's Government, Indigenous Communities, and more. We analyzed three total mining sites using block models, SLCAs, WDMs, and location/cost/environmental considerations.
