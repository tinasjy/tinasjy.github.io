Autonomous Retrieval Claw

Overview

The goal of this project was to design and develop an autonomous retrieval claw to pick up certain objects.

In C++ and using an Arduino UNO board, along with a servo motor and an ultrasonic position sensor, we coded the claw to open and close using a height detection system based on the distance of the sensor from the ground. This made the retrieval of objects much easier than if the claw opened on a timer system.

This project had a main focus on risk identification and analysis. After performing two rounds of C-sketch in our group, we made many prototypes to figure out which materials/structures were most suitable for building this autonomous retrieval system. We then used these models to assess the risks of some materials and moving mechanisms. We ultimately decided to build out system primarily out of cardboard, which proved to be significantly lighter and easier to work with than metal. The only metal pieces we used were for the mouth of the claw so that the thin nature of the sheet metal would allow the claw to reach under any object and pick it up that way. Our cardboard retrieval system proved to be very effective over groups who had only used sheet metal for their designs.

Skills & Technologies

Arduino · Isometric Drawings · C++ · Problem Solving · Risk Identification and Analysis

What I Learned

I learned to connect an Arduino board, servo motor and ultrasonic center and to turn them into a fully working project. In doing so, I gained a better understanding of how simple moving parts, like a servo motor, can be used to operate a claw opening and closing.
